# Enterprise Notes & Limitations

## Assessment status
Cyber Terrafor is an authorized defensive assessment toolkit. It is not a guarantee of vulnerability discovery and should not be marketed as a complete penetration-testing or exploitation framework.

## Evidence model
- **Observed:** directly measurable evidence.
- **Potential:** an indicator that requires further verification.
- **Confirmed:** evidence satisfies the applicable verification criteria.

A fingerprint is intelligence, not proof of an implementation.

## Risk model
Risk is calculated from findings produced by the current analysis. A clean result does not prove complete security. Risk scores should be interpreted with severity, confidence, exposure and business context.

## Network impact
Use the lowest-impact module appropriate for the assessment. Confirm scope and rate limits before testing production systems.

## Third-party dependencies
The package may integrate with external utilities or services. Their licenses, availability, rate limits and terms remain applicable.

## Demo results
Files in `demo/` are synthetic demonstration artifacts. They are not evidence from a real customer or third-party target and must not be represented as such.

## Versioning note
This package is labeled Cyber Terrafor 7.2.0 for the enterprise acquisition bundle. Review the changelog and source before making contractual claims about feature completeness.
